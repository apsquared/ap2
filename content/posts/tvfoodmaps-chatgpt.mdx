[Add TVFoodMaps to ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ac014b3c0188191814246299209ac8e), then start a new chat and try:

- “Use TVFoodMaps to find Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives restaurants in Chicago.”
- “Plan three TV-famous food stops in Austin.”
- “Which TV shows featured these restaurants?”

You’ll get restaurant suggestions, their TV connections, and links to explore more on [TVFoodMaps](https://www.tvfoodmaps.com)—whether you’re planning a weekend getaway or looking for your next local stop.

No TVFoodMaps account is needed. Before heading out, check current hours and confirm the restaurant is open.
